Information We Collect

We may collect the following information.

  1. Publicly available business contact information (company name, representative's name and title, business email address) found on the internet
  2. Information you provide us directly when you contact us, request a free diagnosis, or engage our services (your name, company name, email address, the content of your inquiry, and any materials you share with us)

How We Use It

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  1. To send you marketing information about our AI implementation support service by email
  2. To record requests to stop receiving our emails, so that we do not contact you again
  3. To respond to your inquiries and free-diagnosis requests, and to arrange scheduling
  4. To provide, communicate about, and invoice for services under our agreement with you

We do not use the information for any other purpose.

Use of AI Services

We use AI services (such as Claude and Codex) for part of our work. When information you provide is entered into an AI service, we limit that to what is necessary for the stated purpose, and we work to confirm, through the settings and terms of the service we use, that the input is not used to train that provider's models.

Safeguards

We store collected information with access limited to those who need it, keep it accurate and up to date, and work to delete it promptly once it is no longer needed.

Use of Services Provided Outside Japan

We use cloud and AI services provided by companies based in the United States (Google Workspace, Claude, and Codex), and information you provide may be stored or processed on these services. We cannot identify the specific countries where the servers are located, because these providers operate data centers in multiple countries and we have not selected a storage location (candidates include the United States and other countries where these providers operate data centers).

Having reviewed the Personal Information Protection Commission of Japan's survey of foreign data protection systems, which notes that the United States has no comprehensive federal privacy law and protects personal information through sector-specific and state laws, we apply the safeguards described above.

Requests to Access, Correct, or Stop Use of Your Information

If you would like to request access to, correction of, or a stop to our use of information about you, please contact us at the address below. We will respond after confirming your identity.

Opting Out

If you no longer wish to receive our marketing emails, please reply following the instructions in the email, or contact us at the address below. We will record your request and stop contacting you.
